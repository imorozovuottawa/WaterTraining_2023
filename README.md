Auditory cued reward conditioning behavior

Mice were then head-fixed for simultaneous two-photon imaging and exposed to the conditioned stimulus (a constant 9 kHz auditory tone, 1 s in duration) followed by a 1.5-s delay period and a water reward (~10 µl). All lick times were measured by an infrared beam lick-o-meter and logged using the data acquisition software WaveSurfer (https://wavesurfer.janelia.org/). The ITI between the previous water reward and subsequent CS onset was randomly varied between 60 and 120 s. Each session was 1 hr in duration with 30–35 trials in total. Mice underwent 1 session/day for seven consecutive days. Two-photon calcium image was performed simultaneously on days 1 and 7 of the behavioral task.
